# Never apply

Employers you never apply to or contact, by any route: email, LinkedIn, job boards, jev-job-search.
One per line, as `- Name` or `- Name | why`. Only lines that start with `- ` are read; everything else is explanation.

Matching is employer-wide but by whole name: `- Example` also covers "Example Bank UK" and "Example (Holdings)"
(generic suffix words such as Bank, UK, GmbH, Group, Capital, Holdings), but not "Examplify" or "Big Example Co".
Every tool checks this file: the night job skips the email, the checker flags it, TODAY.html warns, and the
research shift never adds the firm. For jev-job-search, copy the same names into `excludeEmployers` in its
`data/policy.json` (see jev-config/README.md).

Keep your filled-in copy private: it is personal.
